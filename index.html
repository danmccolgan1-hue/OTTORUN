How to Play

Otto the otter swims on his own, following the arrows you turn for him.

Current tile Tap it to rotate it, and it disappears once Otto the otter has swum through it.

Speed boost Yellow arrows that make Otto the otter swim twice as fast for 3 tiles.

Golden tile A tile that stays on the board after Otto the otter crosses it.

Empty tile Open water that costs Otto the otter a heart if he swims onto it.

Clam A tasty treasure that Otto the otter collects by swimming over it.

Heart A rare tile that gives Otto the otter one extra life.

Floating wood A barrier that costs Otto the otter a heart if he bonks into it.

Patch Notes

  • Added a Rock Collection: 54 collectible rocks in five rarities, with a screen to browse them.
  • Rocks now appear on levels 3, 6, 9 and up, always with a safe route to them and on to the next clam.
  • Rocks shake on the board, make a rocky pickup sound and show up on the game-over screens.
  • Added the Dreaming Pebble, an epic rock found only in Zen Mode.
  • Tap a rock in your collection to have Otto hold it while swimming, on the board and on the menu.
  • Renamed the Collection screen and menu button to Rock Collection.
  • Added this Patch Notes window.
  • New level generation: each level has 1 clam plus two crossroads where the next two clams will appear, and a route to all three is checked at the start.
  • Tiles are no longer rearranged to save you from a dead end; only empty water gets new tiles.
  • Otto now speeds up by 3% per tile instead of 5%.
  • Rocks now have a 30% chance per clam from level 3, appear on crossroads, and come once per 3-level stretch (every 10 clams from level 12).
  • The clam and its two future clam crossroads are always more than 3 moves apart.
  • Every level now starts with no empty water tiles.
  • While you have no rocks, level 1 holds a common rock and the tutorial explains it.
  • Longer countdown: 10 seconds on levels 1 to 3 and 7 seconds after that.
  • Added a first-time tutorial on level 1, with a Tutorial On checkbox in How to Play.
  • Rocks you already collected are kept when you update.
